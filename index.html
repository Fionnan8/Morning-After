Support & Help

Morning After is the camera app for nights out with friends — every photo stays locked for the whole group until you unlock them together the next morning. Need a hand? You're in the right place.

Common questions

  • When do photos unlock? Each night has a shared reveal time the next morning. Until then, nobody in the group can see the photos — not even you.
  • Can I delete a photo or night? Yes. Delete any photo or a whole night from inside the app; it removes the stored image, not just the reference.
  • How do I delete all my data? Settings → “Delete my data”. For full account erasure, email us at the address above.
  • Do I need an account? No — you just pick a display name. You can optionally add an email so your nights survive a new phone.
