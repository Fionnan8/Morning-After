Morning After

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Morning After (“the app”) is a night-out photo app: you and your friends take photos that stay locked until a shared “reveal” the next morning. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can remove it.

1. What we collect

  • A display name. Chosen at first launch. No email, phone number, or password is required to use the app.
  • An account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously (no credentials) so your nights and photos can sync across your own devices and be shared with the friends you invite. This creates a random account ID; it is not tied to your real-world identity unless you choose to link an email (below).
  • Photos you take in the app, and metadata about them (who took it, which night it belongs to, when it was taken, whether you’ve flagged it).
  • Night details: a night’s name, its invite code, its members, and its reveal time.
  • An email address, only if you choose to add one. Settings → “Save your account” lets you attach an email so your nights survive a reinstall or a new phone. This is optional. We use it solely to send a one-time verification code and to restore your account later — never for marketing.
  • Standard infrastructure logs (e.g. IP address, request timestamps) generated automatically by our hosting/database provider as part of operating the service. We don’t use these for tracking or profiling.

We do not collect contacts, location, browsing history, or any data from other apps on your device. We do not run ads and do not sell your data.

2. Artificial intelligence

Morning After does not currently use AI to process, analyze, moderate, tag, or generate any of your photos, names, or messages. If that changes in a future version, we will update this section before the feature ships.

3. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf, under their own privacy/security commitments, and don’t use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, and realtime sync). Your account, nights, and photo bytes are stored on Supabase’s infrastructure. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Resend (transactional email). Used only to deliver the one-time code when you choose to link an email address. See resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not share your photos, names, or account data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

4. How photos stay private

Photos are stored in a private bucket — not publicly accessible by URL. Access is enforced by the same rule the app shows you: a photo is visible only to you until your night’s reveal time, after which it becomes visible to the other members of that night. Server-side rules (not just the app’s UI) enforce this, so it can’t be bypassed by tampering with the app.

5. Your data, your control

  • Delete a photo or a whole night any time from inside the app — this removes both the record and the underlying image file from our storage, not just the reference to it.
  • Delete everything at once: Settings → “Delete my data” removes every night you created (and its photos, including the stored image bytes), removes you from any nights you joined as a guest, and signs you out of this device.
  • Full account erasure: because this app has no separate backend server — it talks directly to our database provider under strict per-user access rules — deleting the underlying anonymous account record itself (as opposed to your content) currently requires a request to us directly at the contact below. We’ll process it promptly. We’re working toward making this self-service.

6. Children’s privacy

Morning After is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

7. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what we collect or who we share it with, we’ll update this document and its “Last updated” date, and surface the change in the app.

8. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or concerns: themorningafterapp@gmail.com.
